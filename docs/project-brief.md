## Store requirements

- Phones and tablets only
- All launch products treated as brand new
- Guest checkout without customer accounts
- One physical branch
- Nationwide courier delivery
- Same-day delivery where available
- Store pickup
- Real-time product-variant inventory
- In-stock, low-stock, preorder, and unavailable statuses
- Unavailable products use a Message Us button
- Facebook Messenger as the main contact channel
- Product comparison
- Live search suggestions
- Product thumbnails in search
- Trending searches
- Staff-managed products and homepage content
- Price-drop alerts
- Product images will initially use placeholders and be replaced later

## Launch payments

- Maya online payment
- Cash on store pickup
- Manual bank or e-wallet transfer
- No cash on delivery
- VAT calculated separately
- Do not implement or assume a VAT rate until confirmed
