# GadgetMoTo New Product Intake

## Status

The image audit found 68 unique likely new-product candidates represented by 70
source posters. None is ready for the purchasable catalog. A filename or poster
is not trusted transactional data, and no accompanying approved supplier record,
price list, SKU list, or structured catalog record was found in the repository.

No proposed slug is assigned until the exact marketed product identity is
approved. No official specification lookup was performed because every candidate
is already blocked by missing GadgetMoTo transactional fields.

## Required-data codes

- **M1 — complete catalog intake required:** exact approved product name; exact
  phone/tablet category; unique slug; unique canonical SKU; exact variant name;
  RAM when applicable; storage; current GadgetMoTo selling price; SRP or explicit
  `null`; condition; badge or explicit none; financing availability; active
  status; product ordering decision; verified image approval; and official
  manufacturer specification source.
- **M2 — identity conflict:** resolve the mismatch between the filename and the
  visible product content before any catalog or official-source work.
- **D1 — product decision:** supply and approve the M1 fields, including whether
  the product should be offered by GadgetMoTo.
- **D2 — source selection:** choose which of the two different source posters is
  approved, or approve both as distinct gallery images after content review.
- **D3 — identity decision:** identify the intended product and approve a
  correctly named source image.

All rows have model number, canonical SKU, variant, RAM, storage, current price,
SRP, badge, financing, status, and ordering set to **unverified/missing** unless
and until the M1 intake is supplied.
