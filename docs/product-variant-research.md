# GadgetMoTo Product Variant Research

## Scope and safety

This report audits the live 80-product catalog captured after Migration 20 deployment: 14 Active products and 66 Coming Soon products across 15 current brand labels. Manufacturer research is descriptive only. Migration 19 remains the sole authority for GadgetMoTo availability, and no research import enables a combination.

Official-source priority was Philippines, Southeast Asia or another official region, then global. Physical RAM and extended RAM remain separate. Apple storage-only configurations are recorded with physical RAM not published. Exact color/configuration combinations appear only when an official manufacturer storefront explicitly confirmed the pairing; independent color and storage lists were not multiplied into assumed combinations.

## Launch availability reconciliation

The post-import launch review found one exact, deterministic commercial mapping that was safe to restore: Apple iPhone 17 Black with the existing 256GB commercial configuration. The commercial color and configuration were active, the SKU and positive GadgetMoTo price already existed, and both sides were linked to one manufacturer-confirmed combination. One Migration 19 availability row was therefore added for that exact pair only. No manufacturer-only color or 512GB configuration was enabled, no existing Admin choice was reset, and future availability remains controlled exclusively from the Admin Variant Availability section.

## Commercial and storefront rules

- Manufacturer colors, variants, and exact combinations do not create SKUs, prices, inventory, or availability.
- Mapping is nullable and occurs only for an exact same-product commercial RAM/storage or normalized color match.
- Research-only or incomplete combinations must display **Needs SKU / price** in Admin and remain non-purchasable.
- The storefront may expose an exact color/configuration only when the product is Active, the manufacturer combination is verified, both commercial mappings exist, the commercial variant is active with a nonblank SKU and positive price, and Migration 19 is ON.
- Coming Soon products remain non-purchasable regardless of research completeness.
- No retailer price, fake SKU, invented RAM, invented storage, invented finish, or automatic availability was imported.
